# Teorema 3.7


Sean $A \in \mathbb{K}^{n \times n}$ inversible y $k \in \mathbb{N}$.

$A^{-k} = (A^k)^{-1}$

**Demostración**:

$$
\begin{aligned}
A^{-k} &= (A^{-1})^k \\
&= \prod_{i = 1}^k A^{-1} \\
&= \left( \prod_{i = 1}^k A \right)^{-1} &\text{(por Teorema 3.6)}\\
&= (A^k)^{-1}
\end{aligned}
$$
